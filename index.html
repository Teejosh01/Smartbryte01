Learning beyond examinations.

The world is changing, and the knowledge and skills that young people need to succeed are changing with it. Gone are the days when good grades alone were enough to make a young person ready for the world of work. Today, especially in the age of AI, children need to know how to use technology, think through problems, develop ideas and turn what they know into something the world values.

That's what SmartBryte brings to your child's education.

Alongside their regular academic learning, our students explore technology, learn how to use AI, develop practical digital skills and work on projects that help them put their knowledge to use. They get to discover their interests, build confidence in their abilities and develop skills that can open doors to future opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups can enrol at SmartBryte?

Our programmes are designed for Junior Learners (7-12), Teen Learners (13-19) and Young Adults (20-22).

Does SmartBryte replace conventional schooling?

No. SmartBryte provides supplementary education alongside regular schooling.

What subjects and skills will my child learn?

Students study Science, Technology, English and Mathematics alongside age-appropriate digital skills, including AI, website development, mobile app development, content creation, copywriting, sales and marketing.

How are classes conducted?

Students attend live weekly classes on Zoom, access pre-recorded lessons and take part in monthly physical learning sessions in Lekki, Lagos.

Will my child have a dedicated teacher?

Yes. Every student has an assigned teacher who supports their learning and communicates with parents.

How do you assess progress?

We use tests, examinations, assignments and practical projects to assess learning and development.

How much does it cost?

Tuition is paid every three months. The fee for each programme will be shown alongside its details on the Programmes page.

Will students receive certificates?

Certificates are planned for eligible students who complete programme requirements. Information about the issuing and recognising bodies will be shared once formally confirmed.

How can I enrol?

Choose the most suitable age-based programme and submit an application. Enrolment is open to learners within the eligible age ranges.
